## I

It's a common cliché that we hear all the time: "Be yourself." But how? Most of us, most of the time, are not even sure of who we are.

## II

When I was younger, I used to live by, "Love me or leave me". It was a way to self-soothe, to convince myself that I was worthy of love despite my failings. It left little room for growth or any negotiations. I could be impatient and a procrastinator. I could avoid confrontations and blame my traumas for any hurt I cause - why should I bother to be better?

"I'm a horrible person, you say? Well, tough luck because that's just the way I am." Can you imagine if everyone acted this way? Well, I guess there is no need to imagine. Many people already do.

## III

At some point in our lives, we feel the tendency to stop growing. We become comfortable with one version of ourselves, staying the same and refusing to evolve.

## IV

My friends know that whenever I start a new job, I adopt a new personality. I blame it on being a Gemini, but I've always been fascinated with starting over anew. Isn't it fun to try out different personalities and see which one people will respond better to? But no matter which personality I adopt, I always try to have my core values and beliefs intact.

## V

So, how does one truly be themselves? It begins with understanding the essence of your personality. When you strip away all the pretences, defence mechanisms, and the influence of past traumas, what remains is... you. By silencing the noise around you and embracing quiet introspection, allowing yourself to genuinely feel rather than trying to conform to what you think you should feel, you come closer to connecting with your authentic self. By distinguishing between who you truly are and who others want you to be, you can discover how to live authentically.

There are various paths to being yourself but always remain open and vulnerable. Embrace growth from the depths of your being. Differentiate between your true self and the wounded self. Understand that you are not defined by your past or your parents. Acknowledge your own strengths. Continuously strive to improve and evolve into a better version of yourself. Above all, navigate life with compassion and generosity.
